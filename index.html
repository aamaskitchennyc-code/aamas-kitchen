WELCOME TO AAMA'S

Food that feels like home.

Aama's Kitchen brings the comforting, authentic flavors of traditional Nepalese home cooking to Jackson Heights, NY.

Operating as an extension of the iconic Annapurna Thakali Kitchen, Aama's Kitchen carries forward a proud legacy of culinary excellence, rooted in Himalayan heritage and authentic family recipes.

Named in honor of "Aama" — the Nepali word for mother — our kitchen is built around the warmth, generosity and care of home-cooked meals.
