WELCOME TO AAMA'S

Food that feels like home.

Aama’s Kitchen brings the comforting, authentic flavors of traditional Nepalese home cooking to Jackson Heights, NY. Operating as an extension of the iconic Annapurna Thakali Kitchen, Aama’s Kitchen carries forward a proud legacy of culinary excellence, rooted in rich Himalayan heritage and authentic family recipes. Named in honor of "Aama"—the Nepali word for mother—our kitchen is built on the warmth, generosity, and care of home-cooked meals. Situated in the heart of Jackson Heights at 72-19 Roosevelt Avenue, we serve up classic Nepalese comfort food made from fresh ingredients, fragrant herbs, and traditional spices. From handcrafted Mo:Mo dumplings and savory snacks to hearty traditional Thali platters, every dish offers an authentic taste of Nepal. Whether you are dining in or picking up a meal on the go, Aama’s Kitchen welcomes you with the genuine hospitality of home.
